## What we're building

NeuroViu is an independent neurotechnology company exploring accessible tools for brain-health research, biosignal analysis, and human performance. Our research interests include aging, cognitive decline, brain injury, neurodiversity, and immersive technologies.

### 🌐 Try our free open-source project

**[OpenBrain™](https://github.com/neuroviu/openbrain)** — an interactive educational biosignal explorer with synthetic EEG, ViuGaze™ (EOG), and EMG waveforms. No hardware required.

*Website: [neuroviu.github.io/openbrain](https://neuroviu.github.io/openbrain/) (available after GitHub Pages is enabled).*

### 🔬 Our research software

- **[NeuroViu Lite™ SDK](https://github.com/neuroviu/neuroviu-lite-sdk)** — private, in-development companion SDK for planned NeuroViu Lite hardware; not yet a public release.
- **[NeuroViu Research Agent](https://github.com/neuroviu/neuroviu-research-agent)** — research workflow exploration.
- **[NeuroViu Bridge](https://github.com/neuroviu/neuroviu-bridge)** — offline-first documentation prototype.
- **[NeuroViu Vision Agent](https://github.com/neuroviu/neuroviu-vision-agent)** — computer-vision research prototype.

## Join the community

We welcome students, researchers, educators, and developers to explore OpenBrain, suggest accessible learning features, and contribute original code or documentation. Start with the [OpenBrain repository](https://github.com/neuroviu/openbrain).

> **Research and education only.** Prototypes and simulations are not medical devices and do not provide diagnoses or treatment recommendations.
